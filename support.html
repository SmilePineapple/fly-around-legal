Fly – Support

Fly lets you fly a prop plane, jet, helicopter, drone or hot air balloon over a map of the real world.

Common questions

The map looks blank or blocky. Map pieces download as you fly, so the first moments over a new place need a connection. Flying slower or higher gives them time to arrive.

I bought Fly Pro on another device. Open the Fly Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.

How much free flying do I get? Two minutes a day with the prop plane and the drone. It resets at midnight. Fly Pro removes the limit and unlocks every aircraft.

How do I land? Fly below 800 m and tap Land. Once down, you can save the spot to My places.
